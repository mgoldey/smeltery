# AMBER ff14SB against CHARMM36 pocket charges on PLB tyk2 (issue #29, second system)

Second system for the charge-model comparison that [plb_cdk2.md](plb_cdk2.md) ran on cdk2. Plan, committed before any CHARMM36 SCF (commit `2e333b2`): [plb_tyk2_charmm_plan.md](plb_tyk2_charmm_plan.md).
Same ligands, same 8 poses, same RHF/STO-3G as the merged tyk2 run ([plb_tyk2.md](plb_tyk2.md)); **only the pocket charge model changed**, and the AMBER energies are the stored ones, **not rerun**.
Raw data: `benchmarks/plb/results/tyk2_sto3g_charmm36.json` (the stored AMBER and vacuum energies copied bit for bit from `tyk2_sto3g.json`, plus 104 new CHARMM36 SCFs); statistics:
`benchmarks/plb/results/tyk2_sto3g_charmm36_analysis.json`, recomputed by `tests/test_plb_tyk2_charmm_results.py`, which also checks that the numbers quoted here equal the numbers in that file.

## Answer in four lines

1. **Side by side** (ddE = pose-averaged paired field-interaction difference from the parent; sign flips and pair-order flips between AMBER ff14SB and CHARMM36 on identical geometries):

   | | n analogues | poses | ddE sign flips | pair-order flips | Spearman between models | `charge_sensitivity` floor (kcal/mol) |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | cdk2 | 8 | 5 | 0/8 | 1/28 | +0.976 | 1.26 |
   | tyk2 | 12 | 8 | 0/12 | 0/66 | +1.000 | 0.67 |

2. **The two systems agree on the ranking and disagree on the floor.** On tyk2 the ranking of the 12 analogues by ddE is identical under both models (Spearman +1.000, Kendall +1.000, no pair reordered). The floor (range of ddE(CHARMM36) - ddE(AMBER) over the analogues) is
   0.67 kcal/mol on tyk2, 0.53 times cdk2's 1.26: outside the factor-2 band (0.63 to 2.52) the plan fixed, so by the pre-declared rule the floors **disagree**. The other three criteria of the plan's "agree" (Spearman >= 0.90, flips <= 1 of 12, pair flips <= 6 of 66) are met.
3. **What the floor supports.** A lower bound on the pocket charge model's systematic error for this one model pair: ddE differences of the two series are at least 0.67 (tyk2) and 1.26 (cdk2) kcal/mol away from model to model, so differences below those should be called unranked
   *for this pair of models*. Both numbers are quoted; no single floor is published, no default is set, `FieldInteraction.systematic_floor` is unchanged. A floor from two systems, each a range over 8 or 12 values, is still a lower bound: it can only grow with more ligands, targets and models.
4. **Not shown.** Nothing about Lowdin/Hirshfeld/external ligand-side charges (the three-model comparison #29 asks for), other targets, the water that was dropped, or which model is closer to the truth. The CHARMM36 ddE does not track experiment either (rho +0.252, p 0.4298, same as AMBER's +0.252, p 0.4304).

## What was run

* **Field.** `scripts/plb_pocket_charges.py --ff charmm36` on the same `protein.pdb`, centre (-7.9646, -9.0535, -17.2246) A, cutoff 15 A, ACE/NME deleted (22.7 and 28.3 A from the centre, outside cutoff + 1 A), the same two waters dropped, protonation untouched:
  1013 charges, as for AMBER; histidines HSD x6 and HSE x7, the same tautomers as AMBER's HID/HIE. Net charge of the cropped sphere **+0.895 e** (AMBER +1.402 e, a 0.51 e difference; cdk2's was 1.5 e, so this pair of fields differs less in net charge on tyk2 than on cdk2).
  PQR sha256 `8ff0441b...`, force-field file `charmm36.xml` sha256 `c5eeb1f0...`, OpenMM 8.6.1.
* **SCFs.** 13 ligands x 8 poses = 104 field SCFs, ferric 0.1.0rc7 (the same build as the stored run, VERIFIED identity), 4 workers at `nice -n 10`, one thread each. **All 104 converged**; no pose was dropped; no retry. dE_int(CHARMM36) = E_field(CHARMM36) - the stored E_vac.
* **Geometries identical** by construction and by check: the assembly refuses unless the run's `pose_geometry_sha256` of every ligand, the seed, the jitter, the pivot, the basis and the cutoff equal the stored run's (`run_plb_ddE.fold_base`).
  The AMBER side of this record reproduces the merged run's numbers (rho +0.252, p 0.4304, paired SEM 0.38), as it must.
* **Cost.** 3.02 CPU-h for the 104 SCFs plus a 0.06 CPU-h pilot (2 SCFs, parent and `lig_ejm_48`, pose 1, CHARMM36: 96 and 133 CPU-s; energies discarded), 3.08 CPU-h in all against the 6 CPU-h cap (plan projection 3.5). Nothing is left running.

## Reading it

* **Why the ranking survives.** The pocket models differ by a near-constant shift in the raw interaction energy (+2.38 kcal/mol, spread only 0.27 over ligand-poses); the paired difference cancels that shared part, leaving shifts of at most 0.34 kcal/mol.
  The ddE values themselves are mostly large: the smallest |ddE| is 0.25 (AMBER) and 0.03 (CHARMM36, `lig_ejm_46`), so "0 sign flips" is partly because few ddE sit near zero; `lig_ejm_46` is the near miss (-0.25 to -0.03, same sign).
* **Floor against pose noise.** The tyk2 floor (0.67) is larger than the mean paired pose SEM (0.38 at 8 poses): on this series the charge-model disagreement, not pose sampling, is the larger of the two (point estimates). On cdk2 it was the other way (floor 1.26, SEM 2.07).
* **Why the floors differ is not established.** Candidates the data do not separate: the range is taken over 12 values here and 8 there (it grows with n, which would push tyk2 up, not down); tyk2's ligands are single-site analogues of one scaffold; the net-charge gap between the two fields is smaller; 8 poses against 5. No test of the difference is run, and none would be meaningful from two systems.
* **A lower bound.** Both floors are ranges of a handful of values for one model pair on point-charge pockets cropped identically; adding ligands, targets or charge models can only widen them. Two systems that agree on ranking and differ by a factor of about 2 on the floor give no basis for a default; they give a basis for saying that charge-model sensitivity below about a kcal/mol is measured on both, and not smaller than 0.67 on either.

## What this does not support

* A default `systematic_floor` for `FieldInteraction`, or any statement that 0.67 or 1.26 kcal/mol is the charge-model error of the tier (it is the disagreement of two related protein force fields, which share structure, protonation, crop and the point-charge approximation; they could agree and both be wrong).
* Anything about ligand-side charge models (Lowdin, Hirshfeld, an external model), other targets (the next candidate is another PLB series), a different crop radius, or the dropped waters (two inside the cutoff on tyk2, one on cdk2; not varied).
* That CHARMM36 tracks experiment better or worse than AMBER (rho +0.252 and +0.252, p 0.4298 and 0.4304, n = 12; absence of detection is not absence of an effect). The primary label of `plb_tyk2.md` is unchanged.
* The pooled significance of "two systems agree": none is tested.

## Deviations from the plan

* **D1: `fold_base`.** The plan did not say how the stored AMBER energies would be combined with the new run. `scripts/run_plb_ddE.py assemble --base ... --base-model amber14` was added; it copies the stored vacuum and AMBER energies unchanged (tested) and refuses unless the geometry and QM inputs match.
  The `run` subcommand, which produced the SCFs, was committed unchanged; the run's recorded `smeltery_commit` is the plan's commit (`2e333b2`), before `fold_base` existed.
* **D2: `plb_measure.dielectric_family`.** The run script writes `dielectric_eps = 1.0` into every model's field provenance; the family check then saw two unscreened models (AMBER and CHARMM36) and refused the assembled file. It now treats a model at eps = 1 on its own PQR as a second charge model, not a screening family. Behaviour for the merged tyk2 and cdk2 files is unchanged (their tests pass).
* The secondary "drop poses with any contact under 2.0 A" is vacuous again (the closest contact is 1.04 A; every pose has one), as for AMBER on this series.
* Not done: Lowdin/Hirshfeld/external models, a default-floor constant and test (#29's other acceptance criteria), a third target.

## Decisions for the maintainer

1. Is a floor from two systems that disagree by a factor of about 2 enough to publish anything? This page says no default; both numbers are in `docs/limits.md` (L6, L11).
2. For #29's remaining criteria, ligand-side charge models (Lowdin vs Hirshfeld vs an external model) need no new geometry but do need a different mechanism than the pocket crop: say whether that is the next step or a third pocket target is.
3. Whether to cap the floor claim at "two kinase pockets, one model pair" in the user-facing docs.
